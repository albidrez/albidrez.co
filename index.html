FIELD NOTES 01 OPERATIONS / PRACTICE

01 / The approach

Start with the work.
Not the software.

Understand the work, measure the baseline, and choose the simplest solution that makes a difference.

    1. 01Discover
      01 / Discover
      Interviews+Systems+Documents

      Understand the real work

      We talk with the owner and team, inspect the tools and records they already use, and observe where work gets stuck.

    2. 02Map
      02 / Map
      Trigger→Handoffs→Outcome

      Draw the real workflow

      We document each handoff, workaround, and decision—who owns it, how long it takes, and where the delays happen. This gives us a baseline.

    3. 03Simplify
      03 / Simplify
      DeletePlain codeAI agentHuman

      Choose the right treatment

      Every step earns its place: eliminate it, automate it with simple code, consider AI where judgment is needed, or keep a person in control.

    4. 04Build
      04 / Build
      Small pilot→Test→Deploy

      Start small, then put it to work

      We test a focused solution in the real workflow, using existing tools where possible. We expand only when it proves useful.

    5. 05Refine
      05 / Refine
      Before→After→Adjust

      Prove and improve the result

      We compare the new workflow with the baseline—time saved, errors reduced, or revenue captured—and keep refining with the people who use it.

    → Revenue. Efficiency. Better margins.
